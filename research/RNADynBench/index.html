A traceable data foundation.

01 / Curate

Experimental RNA structures from RNA-Solo, with molecular context checked against original PDB/mmCIF records.

02 / Simulate

All-atom MD with Amber RNA OL3 and OPC water, at 300 K, 1 bar and 150 mM NaCl.

03 / Validate

Production completion, coordinate continuity, topology and multi-chain behavior checks, with retained diagnostic records.
