What is available?

Each trajectory folder contains rna.xtc, rna.gro and rna.pdb. The Box release also includes metadata, split files, simulation protocol files, manifest.csv and SHA256SUMS.

Open a sample folder from the search results to download individual files or the complete sample. Download XTC together with its matching GRO or PDB.
